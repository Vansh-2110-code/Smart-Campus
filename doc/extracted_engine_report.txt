Architectural Findings & Production Selection Rationale:
1. Why One-Class SVM Was Rejected: One-Class SVM delivered the weakest discriminative capacity (ROC-AUC 0.8444) and
missed almost half of all anomalies (48.25% False Negative Rate). Crucially, its quadratic training scaling O(N²) renders frequent
continuous retraining on large student bodies computationally unviable.
2. Why Standalone Isolation Forest Was Insufficient: While achieving high precision (91.76%), standalone Isolation Forest
suffered a 41.61% False Negative Rate on isolated deterministic violations (e.g. impossible transit across distant perimeter gates).
3. Why the Hybrid Ensemble Wins: The Hybrid Calibrated Ensemble pairs the 100% recall of deterministic physical invariant
rules with the nuanced pattern recognition of Isolation Forest. It achieved the highest overall ROC-AUC (0.9672) and F1-Score
(0.9132) while restricting false alarms to an outstanding 0.64%.

8. Hardware Dependencies & Edge Mitigation Roadmap
 Analog Match-Confidence Telemetry: Current schema accepts boolean (entry/denied). When optical/capacitive readers are
commissioned, the numeric match confidence (0.00 – 1.00) will immediately slot in as Feature #11 in our Isolation Forest to catch
worn cards or smudged scanner glass before outright denial occurs.
 Biometric Liveness & Anti-Spoofing: Vendor hardware liveness telemetry (capacitive pulse, subdermal infrared) will feed into
the scoring pipeline without modifying API contracts.
 Network Disconnects & Store-and-Forward Replay: Offline turnstiles buffer transactions and dump them in bursts upon
reconnection. The service compares event timestamp against ingestion time; events with delta > 300s are tagged as backfills to
update historical rolling averages without firing false velocity alarms.
